  <action>
    **Extend `src/lib/teams/queries.ts`** with new query functions:

    1. **`getPlayerStats(teamId, leagueId, season)`** - Aggregates player statistics from fixture_events:
       - First, get all finished fixture IDs for the team in the season
       - Two queries approach (per RESEARCH.md):
         a. Scorers and cards: GROUP BY playerId on fixtureEvents WHERE teamId = our team AND fixtureId IN our fixtures. Counts: goals (type IN 'goal', 'penalty_scored'), yellowCards (type = 'yellow_card'), redCards (type = 'red_card'), appearances (COUNT DISTINCT fixtureId).
         b. Assists: COUNT goal events WHERE assistPlayerId IS NOT NULL AND teamId = our team, GROUP BY assistPlayerId.
       - Join with players table to get name, position, photoUrl, number
       - Merge results into unified player stats array: `Array<{ playerId, name, position, photoUrl, number, goals, assists, yellowCards, redCards, appearances }>`
       - **Important**: Assists count is on events where the player is assistPlayerId on a goal/penalty_scored event for their team (NOT where playerId matches -- the assist is attributed to assistPlayerId)

    2. **`getTeamFixtures(teamId, leagueId, season, options?: { recentLimit?: number; upcomingLimit?: number })`** - Fetches recent results (last N finished) and upcoming fixtures (next N scheduled) for the team. Uses alias() for homeTeam/awayTeam double-join (established pattern). Returns two arrays: `{ recent: FixtureWithTeams[]; upcoming: FixtureWithTeams[] }` where each fixture has: id, matchweek, kickoff, status, homeScore, awayScore, venue, homeTeam (id, name, shortName, logoUrl, slug), awayTeam (same). Defaults: 10 recent, 5 upcoming. Include team slugs in the select.

    3. **`getOpponentPositions(leagueId, season)`** - Fetches the current (latest matchweek) position of every team in the league. Returns `Map<number, number>` (teamId -> position). Used for fixture difficulty colouring.

      - Players sorted by appearances desc within each group
      - **Appearances bar**: Horizontal bar per player showing percentage of total team fixtures as a CSS percentage-width bar. Label shows appearance count. This is the "minutes distribution" proxy (per RESEARCH.md -- minutes not available, use appearances).
      - Position from players.position: 'GK', 'DEF', 'MID', 'FWD'. Players with null/unknown position go into a separate "Other" section.
    - Responsive: Callout cards stack on mobile (1 per row). Roster tables have horizontal scroll on mobile.
